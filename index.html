About me

From pixels
to voices
to agents

I started in computer vision research at KLE Tech's Center of Excellence in Visual Intelligence, restoring images ruined by lens flare, low light and murky water. That work became a first-author paper at PReMI 2023 and an entry in the MIPI challenge at CVPR 2023.

Then I spent a year and a half at SandLogic in Bengaluru, first as a deep learning intern and then as an AI developer. I helped build Ira, a voice agent that went to production and makes sales, plus RAG systems and document AI for clients in insurance and other sectors.

Now I'm in Germany doing my M.Sc., building agent systems that remember the right things, test themselves with evals, and keep a human in control. In 2026 my team topped the Krones Vision AI leaderboard and won a 24-hour hackathon.

I trained five interns from scratch at SandLogic and handed my projects over to them. I like explaining things almost as much as building them.

Voice-first in-car assistant with per-driver memory

MayorDesk

AI desk for town offices flooded with information requests

Town offices must answer every citizen request by law, and mass requests are used to pressure mayors. Nothing is sent without a human click.

Krones Vision AI

Industrial defect inspection

35,000 grayscale images with 134,000 polygon annotations across 27 defect types. I led modelling in a three-person team: a five-fold EfficientNetV2-S ensemble chosen by cross-validation, not by the leaderboard. FP16 ONNX export made inference about twice as fast. TensorRT was benchmarked and dropped because the gain didn't justify it.

PReMI 2023Springer, first author

DeFlare-Net: Flare Detection and Removal Network

Removes lens flare from images while keeping the light source itself, which earlier methods erased along with the flare. A light-source detection module and a flare removal network, trained with a new loss that combines flare, light-source and reconstruction terms.

Read on Springer
CVPR 2023Workshops, co-author

MIPI 2023 Challenge on Nighttime Flare Removal: Methods and Results

Our team, CEVI Explorers, entered DeFlare-Net and placed 9th of 11 final teams, from 120 registered. The winners were from Xiaomi and Samsung Research.

Read the paper (PDF)
LLIE-NetSenior design project

Low-light image enhancement for 3D reconstruction

I led modelling in a four-person team. A U-Net enhances the lightness channel of the image; on the LOL benchmark it reached PSNR 27.09 and SSIM 0.895, ahead of UHDFour (23.09). The enhanced images then fed 3D reconstruction.

DeepSwin-UNetStudent research project

Swin Transformer for underwater image restoration

Restoring colour and contrast in underwater images, trained on synthetic data I generated with the lab's image formation model. This also became my bachelor's thesis topic.
